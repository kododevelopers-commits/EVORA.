Our Story

Crafted to Wear, Built to Last

From a single sketchbook to a wardrobe staple across the country — here's the story behind Evora.

Who We Are

Quiet design, honest fabrics

Evora is a modern clothing label built on the idea that everyday wear should feel as good as it looks. Every piece is designed in-house, cut from quality fabrics, and finished with the kind of details that hold up to real life — wash after wash, season after season.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Quality Fabrics

Hand-picked cottons, linens and denim — sourced for feel, not just for looks.

Honest Pricing

No middlemen, no markups. You pay for the cut, the fabric, and the work — not the label.

Made to Last

Timeless fits, sturdy stitching, and washes that keep their shape — designed to be worn for years.

Our Journey

From Sketchbook to Streetwear

2019

The First Capsule

Evora started as a six-piece capsule — tees and a shirt, designed at a kitchen table and stitched in small batches.

2021

Our Studio Opens

Demand grew fast enough to open a small Karachi studio — a fitting room, a fabric wall, and a growing team.

2023

Shop Goes Online

We took the collection online so anyone, anywhere, could build their everyday wardrobe with Evora.

2025

Unisex & Core Staples

From relaxed unisex staples to refined core pieces, we expanded the line without losing the clean Evora feel.

Today

Still Designing, Still Stitching

Same studio, same standards — just more styles, more sizes, and more reasons to open your wardrobe.
